About Us

Meet the person behind Jharkhand's trusted weighing solutions

Fazal Murtaza

Proprietor, New Jharkhand Scale

New Jharkhand Scale (NJS) is a Ranchi-based weighing solutions company led by Fazal Murtaza. Since 2018, we have been specializing in the manufacturing and supply of digital pitless weighbridges, electronic weighing scales, CCTV monitoring systems, and advanced weighbridge management software. We serve industries, mining operations, and logistics businesses across Jharkhand and Bihar with reliable, accurate, and durable weighing solutions built on SAIL steel structures and Capital brand load cells.
